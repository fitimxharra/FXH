FXH CAM Public Beta — Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Public Beta: this test version is free through 31 December 2026. Camera capture features are scheduled to deactivate on 1 January 2027. Gallery access remains available so you can download media stored on your device.

What is stored

FXH CAM Public Beta currently does not use an active cloud account or cloud photo backup.

  • Photos and videos created in the app are stored locally in your browser/app storage on your device.
  • Your profile name, preferences and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Your profile avatar, if you choose one, is stored locally on your device.

These items are not uploaded to a central FXH CAM user database in this Public Beta.

Camera access

The app requests camera access only so you can capture photos and video. Live camera content is not transmitted to an FXH CAM server.

Local data and deletion

Because Beta media and profile information are stored locally, clearing the site/app data, clearing browser storage, uninstalling the PWA, or changing to another domain may remove or make that local data inaccessible. Download any media you want to keep before clearing site data or uninstalling.

After the Public Beta

From 1 January 2027, this Beta is designed to disable camera capture while keeping Gallery available for local media export. The future official FXH CAM may use a different domain and may have different account, storage and privacy terms.

Feedback & contact

Help us improve FXH CAM through the in-app Public Beta feedback form. The receiving address is not displayed in the app.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the Beta evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
