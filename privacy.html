Analog Cam ("the app") is a film-camera simulator. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can remove it.

Information we collect

If you create an account, we collect and store:

  • Your email address (used for sign-in)
  • The username and full name you choose
  • Basic subscription status (trial or active), if you subscribe

This account data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and is never sold or shared with advertisers.

Photos and videos you take in the app are stored locally on your own device (in your browser's local storage). They are not uploaded to our servers unless a future cloud-sync feature is explicitly turned on by you.

If you subscribe, payment is processed by our payment provider (Stripe). We do not see or store your full card details — only a subscription status and a reference id are kept on our side.

Camera access

The app requests access to your device camera solely to let you take photos and record video within the app. Camera video is never transmitted anywhere or recorded without your explicit action (pressing the shutter/record button).

How to delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from inside the app: go to Profile → Delete Account and confirm. This immediately and permanently removes your account record from our database.

If you're unable to access the app, you can request deletion by emailing [FILL IN YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address associated with your account. We'll delete it within 30 days.

Data retention

Account data is kept for as long as your account exists. Locally-stored photos/videos remain on your device until you delete them or clear your browser's site data.

Children's privacy

Analog Cam is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
